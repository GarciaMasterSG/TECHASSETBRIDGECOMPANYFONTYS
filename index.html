THE FINANCING MISMATCH

Promising technology. Expensive machinery. Limited cash-flow history.

Deep-tech SMEs can need specialised machinery before commercial revenue becomes predictable. Traditional underwriting may not fit that development path, and available financing instruments can be difficult to compare.

01

Equipment needs come early

Prototype-to-production transitions can require significant capital expenditure before steady sales.

02

Risk evidence is fragmented

Technical milestones, customer validation, collateral and guarantees are rarely presented in one decision-ready view.

03

Applications take time

Companies can pursue unsuitable financing routes before understanding the lender's real decision criteria.

WHAT WE PROPOSE

One focused equipment-financing readiness process.

Not another investor directory. A structured preparation service designed to help SMEs and qualified finance partners have more productive conversations.

1

Describe the asset

What equipment is needed, why now, and what milestones will it unlock?

2

Map the risk

Capture revenue readiness, technical evidence, supplier terms and possible guarantees.

3

Compare pathways

Identify questions to explore around leasing, vendor finance, bank debt and export credit.

4

Prepare the case

Create a concise lender discussion brief and identify missing evidence before outreach.

Where we add value: Our initial focus is the evidence and structuring work before a financing decision. Financing decisions remain with authorised lenders and partners.

RESEARCH PILOT

Questions we want to investigate
