TECHASSET BRIDGE — EQUIPMENT FINANCING PREPARATION CHECKLIST
=============================================================

Use this checklist to collect the documents and information that financiers
commonly ask about before you approach a bank, lessor or other provider.
Requirements differ per provider, so always confirm what they need.

General information only — not financial, legal or tax advice. TechAsset
Bridge is not a bank, lender or provider of guaranteed financing.


1. THE EQUIPMENT
----------------
[ ] Formal supplier quotation (price, delivery time, payment schedule)
[ ] Technical description of the equipment and its specifications
[ ] Expected useful life and maintenance requirements
[ ] Warranty and service terms
[ ] Information on resale or re-use potential (second-hand market)
[ ] Any supplier buy-back or vendor-finance options offered


2. THE PURPOSE
--------------
[ ] What the equipment enables (production step, order, milestone)
[ ] Why it is needed now, and what happens if it is delayed
[ ] Milestone plan linking the equipment to technical and commercial progress


3. THE COMPANY
--------------
[ ] Latest annual accounts
[ ] Recent interim figures (e.g. last quarter)
[ ] Short company profile: team, technology, customers, stage
[ ] Overview of existing loans, leases and securities given
[ ] Overview of previous financing attempts and their outcome


4. COMMERCIAL EVIDENCE
----------------------
[ ] Customer orders, letters of intent or framework agreements
[ ] Pilot results or customer validation
[ ] Relevant certifications or test results


5. FINANCIAL SCENARIOS
----------------------
[ ] Cash-flow forecast including the financing costs
[ ] Base case and a cautious (downside) scenario
[ ] Explanation of how delays would be handled


6. RISK MITIGATION
------------------
[ ] Own contribution and how it is funded
[ ] Available collateral
[ ] Possible guarantees or risk-sharing schemes (check eligibility per scheme)
[ ] Subsidies, grants or co-financing already secured or applied for


7. BEFORE THE MEETING
---------------------
[ ] One consistent summary of the case that all documents agree with
[ ] A list of questions for each financier (see the lender discussion pack
    template)
